About MetaTech

Two decades of precision, reliability, and engineering excellence in telecommunications infrastructure across the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia.

—  OUR STORY

From 2005 to Today

Established on the enduring foundation and proven success of Mazaya Al Adaa Contracting Co. Ltd., METATECH INNOVATIONS represents the next strategic phase of growth and innovation in telecommunications and fiber optic infrastructure services.

Guided by a team of highly qualified managers, seasoned engineers, and skilled technical professionals, we offer comprehensive, end-to-end solutions encompassing survey and design, civil construction and FOC installation, testing, commissioning, and managed network services. The company specializes in the implementation of Long-Haul, Metro, FTTH (Fiber to the Home), and OSP (Outside Plant) fiber optic networks across all scales and levels of complexity.

Through the rich heritage and extensive experience inherited from Mazaya Al Adaa Contracting Co. Ltd. — a company that has successfully completed over 250 projects, installed more than 10,000 kilometers of fiber optic networks, served upwards of 25 clients, and executed projects across five or more regions within the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia — METATECH INNOVATIONS is firmly committed to upholding the same unwavering standards of quality, safety, innovation, and customer satisfaction that have long defined its success.
